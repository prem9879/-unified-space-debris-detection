System thesis

Physics-first. AI-second. Human judgement always stays in the loop.

We shortlist objects with orbital mechanics, then let deep models rank risk, trajectory drift, and uncertainty. That keeps the system defensible when the network is noisy or the feed is incomplete.

01

Live ingestion

CelesTrak, Space-Track, and local imagery flow into a canonical catalog with epoch, provenance, and shell tags.

02

Orbital features

Semi-major axis, altitude, inclination, drag proxies, cyclic angles, and relative-distance cues are normalized for learning.

03

Multimodal AI

LSTM / GRU and Transformer models handle sequence prediction, while XGBoost and CNN / YOLO provide fast, interpretable baselines.

04

Collision engine

Physics gating, relative-velocity checks, and learned scores fuse into a low / medium / high risk band with audit-ready reasoning.

05

Real-time delivery

Streaming updates are pushed into alerts, dashboards, and exportable research packs without blocking the operator flow.

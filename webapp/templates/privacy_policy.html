Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

We collect account information (email, authentication events), product telemetry (page and event analytics), and operational data needed to run this platform. We do not sell personal information.

What We Collect

  • Account data: email address, password hash, verification state.
  • Security signals: login attempts, rate-limit outcomes, audit events.
  • Usage data: page views, event tracking metadata, feature interactions.
  • Billing data: subscription plan and payment outcome status from the configured gateway.

How We Use Data

  • Deliver authentication, account recovery, and access control.
  • Protect platform integrity with abuse detection and rate limiting.
  • Improve product experience with aggregate analytics.
  • Process subscriptions and lifecycle changes.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management and optional analytics cookies with consent where required.

Your Rights

You can request account data export, correction, or deletion by contacting the operators of this deployment.
